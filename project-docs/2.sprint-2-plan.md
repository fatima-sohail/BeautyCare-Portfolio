# Sprint 2 Plan

## Sprint Goal

Complete the remaining planned features that help customers find haircare and makeup products.

## Selected User Stories

- Frizz-free hair
- Color-treated haircare
- Inspiration-based product suggestions

## Sprint Capacity

- Total story points: 23
- Focus: Remaining prioritized work and additional customer needs

## Planning Rationale

Sprint 2 has more story points than Sprint 1 but Sprint 1 has more value.

Sprint 1 focuses on the tasks that are most important to customers and the business while Sprint 2 includes more work overall.

Story points show the amount of effort needed for a task. Value shows how helpful or important that task is.
