# Sprint 1 Retrospective Follow-Up Email after retrospective meeting


**To:** Scrum Team  
**From:** Scrum Master  
**Subject:** Sprint 1 Retrospective: Key Takeaways and Next Steps

Dear Scrum Team,

Thank you for your time, collaboration, and effort throughout Sprint 1. I appreciate everyone’s contribution to the work completed during the sprint and to the discussion during our retrospective.

Thank you as well for participating in the Sprint 1 Retrospective. This email summarizes the work completed during the sprint, the challenges identified, and the next steps for Sprint 2.

During Sprint 1, two user stories were completed:

- Minimalist Skincare
- Skin Concern Selection

The Foundation for Oily Skin user story was not completed because the required foundation product data was not yet ready.

## Key Takeaways

- A dry-skin selection option and a simple skincare routine were created to help busy customers find cleanser, moisturizer, and sunscreen more easily.
- Skin-concern selection options and product recommendations were created to help customers find products based on their skincare needs.
- Clear explanations were added to show how recommended products support customers’ selected skin concerns.
- Customer feedback is needed to confirm whether the recommended basic skincare routine includes the products customers consider most important.
- Customer testing is needed to make sure the skincare recommendations are clear, easy to find, and easy to understand.
- Foundation product information still needs to be collected and connected to the filter so customers can receive accurate recommendations for oily skin.

## Sprint 2 Focus

For Sprint 2, the team will focus on completing the Foundation for Oily Skin story and using customer feedback to improve skincare recommendations.

Thank you again for your thoughtful contributions and commitment to improving the product experience. I look forward to building on these learnings in Sprint 2.
