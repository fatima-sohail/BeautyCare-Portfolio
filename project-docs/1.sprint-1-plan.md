# Sprint 1 Plan

## Sprint Goal

Deliver the highest value beauty product discovery features within the available sprint capacity.

## Selected User Stories

- Minimalist skincare
- Skin concern selection
- Foundation for oily skin

## Sprint Capacity

- Total story points: 21
- Focus: High-value customer needs across skincare and makeup

## Planning Rationale

Sprint 1 prioritizes high-value stories that provide meaningful customer benefits while remaining 
feasible within the planned capacity. The goal is to deliver the strongest initial product increment
and collect feedback before planning additional work.
