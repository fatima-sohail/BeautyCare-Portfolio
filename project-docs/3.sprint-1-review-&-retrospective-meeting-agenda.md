# Sprint 1 Review and Retrospective Meeting Agenda

## Purpose

This document provides the agenda and discussion questions for the Sprint 1 Review and Sprint 1 Retrospective meetings.

The Sprint Review focuses on reviewing completed work, gathering stakeholder or customer feedback, and discussing the value delivered during Sprint 1.

The Sprint Retrospective focuses on reflecting on the team’s process, identifying what went well, discussing challenges, and agreeing on improvements for Sprint 2.

---

# Sprint 1 Review

## Agenda

1. Welcome and purpose of the Sprint Review
2. Presentation of completed work, including the Product Backlog, acceptance criteria, and prototypes
3. Stakeholder or customer feedback
4. Discussion of Sprint 1 goals and value delivered
5. Next steps and planning for Sprint 2

## Discussion Questions

1. Does the Product Backlog reflect the most important features for customers?
2. Are the acceptance criteria clear and aligned with user needs?
3. What feedback do you have on the early designs or prototypes?
4. Did the sprint deliver the expected value?
5. Are there any changes that should be made to the Product Backlog before Sprint 2?

---

# Sprint 1 Retrospective

## Agenda

1. Welcome and purpose of the Sprint Retrospective
2. Discussion of what went well during Sprint 1
3. Discussion of challenges, obstacles, or blockers
4. Identification of improvements for Sprint 2
5. Agreement on action items and next steps

## Discussion Questions

1. What helped the team work effectively during Sprint 1?
2. What slowed the team down or caused confusion?
3. How can customer feedback be included more effectively in the next sprint?
4. What changes should be made to the team’s process or communication?
5. What action items should the team complete before or during Sprint 2?
